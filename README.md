# Join us in rebuilding the Python community and continuing the mission of empowering diverse women to excel in technology careers.

## History
We are a team of legacy volunteers and leaders who were previously affiliated with Women Who Code Python Technical Track. Please join us in rebuilding community and continuing the mission of empowering diverse women to excel in technology careers.

On April 18th, 2024, Women Who Code’s CEO and Board of Directors announced the organization is immediately dissolved. Read more about their press release https://womenwhocode.com/blog/the-end-of-an-era-women-who-code-closing

Become a member or add yourself to our legacy spinoff community list on Github
https://github.com/gravitytrope/wwcode-legacygroups

## Our Mission
This group is dedicated to women in software development and programming in Python. Whether you are a beginner looking to improve your skills or an advanced coder interested in exploring new techniques, our meetups will provide a supportive and engaging environment for you to learn and grow.

Join us for hands-on workshops, collaborative coding sessions, and networking opportunities with like-minded women in the tech community.
